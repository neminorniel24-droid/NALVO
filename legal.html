Terms of Use

  1. What Nalvo is. Nalvo is a free directory where business owners list their own businesses in Tamil Nadu and Kerala, and the public finds them. We do not sell goods or services listed here and are not a party to any deal between a customer and a business.
  2. Your account. You sign in with your email. Keep access to that email safe. You are responsible for what you post under your account.
  3. Your listing. You must be the owner or an authorised person for the business. Details, photos and licence numbers must be true, current and yours to publish. Do not post other people's photos or personal data without permission.
  4. Permission to display. You allow Nalvo to display your listing (including the phone number and photo you provide) on the website, and to hide or remove it under these rules. You keep ownership of your content.
  5. Review and moderation. New and edited listings are reviewed before they appear. We may reject, edit, hide or remove any listing, and block accounts, especially for breaking the Prohibited Listings rules or after reports. We may do this without notice where needed to protect people.
  6. Verified badge. "Verified by Nalvo" means we checked the business in person or by documents on the date we checked. It is not a guarantee of quality, price or future conduct.
  7. No guarantee. Listings are provided by owners. We do not promise accuracy, availability or results. Check details and licences yourself before paying or visiting.
  8. Limits of liability. To the extent allowed by law, Nalvo is not liable for losses from dealings between users and listed businesses, or from downtime or errors. Nothing here removes rights you have under Indian law.
  9. Misuse. Do not scrape the site, attack or test its security without written permission, or use it to harass others. To report a security weakness, email us (see below) and give us reasonable time to fix it.
  10. Changes and law. We may update these terms. Continued use means you accept the updated terms. These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts in Tamil Nadu have jurisdiction.

Privacy Policy

  • What we collect: your email (for sign-in), and the listing details you enter: business name, category, area, phone number, address, timings, description, licence number, photo, and optionally the location coordinates you choose to save.
  • What is public: everything in an approved listing, including the business phone number, address, licence number, photo and location, is visible to anyone. Do not enter personal information you do not want public. Your sign-in email is not shown publicly.
  • Why: to run the directory, review listings, prevent misuse and contact you about your account or listing.
  • "Near me": if a visitor taps it, their browser asks permission to use their location. We use it only on their device to sort results. We do not store visitor locations.
  • Where data is kept: with our hosting providers (database and storage on Supabase, website on Netlify), which may store data outside India.
  • Sharing: we do not sell your data. We may share information when the law requires it or to deal with illegal activity.
  • Storage on your device: the site may remember your chosen language in your browser, and the sign-in service stores a session token.
  • Your choices: you can edit or delete your listing on the site. To delete your account and data, or to ask what we hold, email us. We will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Children: Nalvo is not meant for people under 18 to run listings.

Prohibited listings

The following are not allowed on Nalvo. Listings that break these rules are removed and accounts may be blocked.

  • Illegal drugs and narcotics, or anything that helps buy or sell them
  • Weapons, firearms, ammunition, explosives, or illegal fireworks
  • Stolen, counterfeit or pirated goods
  • Gambling, betting, lotteries, matka, and online money games
  • Alcohol or tobacco sales without a valid licence
  • Adult services, escort services or anything sexual
  • Fake jobs, loan or investment schemes, unregistered chit funds, "double your money" offers
  • Fake certificates, degrees, marksheets, licences or documents
  • Medical, pharmacy, ayurveda or other health services without the registration required by law, and miracle-cure claims
  • Protected wildlife or forest products (for example ivory or animal skins)
  • Anything that exploits or endangers children
  • Hate speech, harassment, threats, or impersonating another person or business
  • Money transfer or "hawala" services and other unlicensed financial services

Some categories ask for a licence or registration number (for example FSSAI for food, drug licence for pharmacies, seed or pesticide licence for farm supplies). Rules and licences differ, so owners are responsible for following the law that applies to them.

Report a listing or contact our grievance officer

  • On the site: sign in and use the "Report" link on any listing. Listings reported by several different people are hidden until we review them.
  • By email: Nemin Orniel, Grievance Officer, neminorniel24@gmail.com. Include the listing name and area, and what is wrong.
  • Our response: we aim to acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and act promptly on illegal content.
  • Serious cases: for crime or immediate danger, call the police (112). For online financial fraud, call 1930 or use cybercrime.gov.in.
